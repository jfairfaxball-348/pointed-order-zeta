# Pointed Order Zeta: Artin–Kummer Spectra of Reduction Orders

**Status: PRIOR_ART_REFRAME**

This repository is a high-risk conjecture / object-discovery project. It investigates whether a simple arithmetic statistic—the index of the subgroup generated by a point after reduction modulo primes—supports a useful zeta/spectral formalism connecting multiplicative order, Artin phenomena, Kummer extensions, Chebotarev theory, analytic number theory, algebraic groups, Galois representations, 1-motives, and possibly motivic or cohomological structures.

The proposed zeta function and the proposed “spectrum” are **experimental research objects**. Their novelty and mathematical value have **not** been established. No priority claim is made.

## Frozen starting object: the \(\mathbf G_m\) case

Let \(a\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0,\pm1\}\). Initially assume that \(a\) is not a perfect power. For a prime \(p\nmid a\), let

\[
\operatorname{ord}_p(a)
\]

denote the multiplicative order of \(a\bmod p\), and define the residual index

\[
I_a(p)=\frac{p-1}{\operatorname{ord}_p(a)}
       =[\mathbf F_p^\times:\langle a\bmod p\rangle].
\]

Thus \(I_a(p)=1\) exactly when \(a\) is a primitive root modulo \(p\).

The frozen starting Euler product is

\[
Z_a(s,z)=\prod_{p\nmid a}
\left(1-I_a(p)^{-z}p^{-s}\right)^{-1}.
\]

For initial rigorous work, use real \(z\ge 0\) and \(\Re(s)>1\). Do not assume analytic continuation.

The provisional order spectrum is

\[
\Delta_a(z)=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{\pi(x)}
\sum_{\substack{p\le x\\p\nmid a}} I_a(p)^{-z},
\]

whenever the limit exists. “Artin–Kummer order spectrum” and “order spectrum” are provisional names and are not frozen terminology.

## Session-2 foundational result

Session 2 established, for \(n\ge1\), the exact finite-group criterion
\[
n\mid I_a(p)
\iff
n\mid p-1
\quad\text{and}\quad
a\bmod p\in(\mathbf F_p^\times)^n
\]
for \(p\nmid a\). Consequently, for \(p\nmid an\),
\[
n\mid I_a(p)
\iff
p\text{ splits completely in }
K_n(a)=\mathbf Q(\zeta_n,a^{1/n}).
\]

For each fixed \(n\), Chebotarev gives density
\[
\frac{1}{[K_n(a):\mathbf Q]}
\]
for the primes with \(n\mid I_a(p)\), up to the finite exceptional set. This fixed-\(n\) result does **not** by itself justify exchanging an infinite Möbius expansion with a prime-density limit.

The non-perfect-power assumption is not needed for the individual splitting equivalence; it remains relevant to field-degree formulas and later constants. Entanglement belongs to degree/tower and simultaneous-condition questions, not to the fixed-\(n\) equivalence.

See notes/foundational-derivation.md for the complete derivation and caveats.

## Verified endpoint and Euler-product checks

At \(z=0\),
\[
Z_a(s,0)=\zeta(s)\prod_{p\mid a}(1-p^{-s}),
\]
when precisely the primes dividing \(a\) are omitted.

For \(z\ge0\) and \(\Re(s)>1\), the logarithmic expansion
\[
\log Z_a(s,z)=\sum_{p\nmid a}\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{I_a(p)^{-zk}}{k p^{ks}}
\]
is absolutely convergent by comparison with \(\log\zeta(\Re(s))\).

As \(z\to+\infty\), each Euler-factor weight satisfies
\(I_a(p)^{-z}\to1\) for \(I_a(p)=1\) and tends to \(0\) otherwise. Any limit involving the full infinite product or an infinite Kummer-degree series still requires an independent interchange argument.

## Analytic target, not theorem

Starting from

\[
\log Z_a(s,z)=\sum_p\sum_{k\ge1}
\frac{I_a(p)^{-zk}}{k p^{ks}},
\]

later work will test whether the leading singular behaviour near \(s=1^+\) is governed by \(\Delta_a(z)\), potentially via a regular factor of the form

\[
Z_a(s,z)\sim H_a(s,z)(s-1)^{-\Delta_a(z)}.
\]

This is a research target, not a theorem.

## Novelty policy

The first responsibility is to determine whether the proposed object is already known under another name, an immediate repackaging of generalized-Artin machinery, new but trivial, or nontrivial and worth developing.

A mathematically equivalent treatment under different notation counts as prior art. In particular, later auditing must distinguish residual-index distributions and general averages from the continuous \(z\)-parameter, the two-variable Euler-product packaging, the proposed singularity exponent, possible Kummer/L-function factorisations, and pointed-algebraic-group or 1-motivic extensions.

No public priority claim is permitted during the pilot.

## Current exit state

**Exit state: PRIOR_ART_REFRAME**

Session 3 found direct prior-art collisions for the residual-index distribution and general weighted averages, and an immediate-corollary collision for the family \(f_z(n)=n^{-z}\): Felix--Murty's fixed-base generalized-Artin theorem gives \(\Delta_a(z)\) and the same Kummer-degree constant under GRH for each fixed real \(z>0\). Wagstaff's degree formula gives the required absolute convergence, and Akbary--Fakhari supplies product/entanglement structure for the corresponding constants.

The project survives only in reframed form. Session 4 should treat the generalized-Artin mean as known input and test stronger analytic structure of the normalized infinite-Kummer weighted Euler product, such as continuation/nonvanishing, complex-\(z\) dependence, or a Kummer-tower Artin/Dedekind-\(L\) factorization. No novelty claim is made for this survivor.
