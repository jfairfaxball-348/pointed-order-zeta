# Generalisation Map

This file records distant directions without authorizing work on them.

## Level 0: \(\mathbf G_m\) with a point

Current prototype: a fixed \(a\in\mathbf Q^\times\) represented initially by a suitable integer, with residual index in \(\mathbf F_p^\times\).

Question: does this prototype carry nontrivial structure beyond established generalized-Artin machinery?

## Level 1: elliptic curve with a rational point

For \(E/\mathbf Q\) and infinite-order \(P\in E(\mathbf Q)\), a provisional analogue at good primes is
\[
I_{E,P}(p)=[E(\mathbf F_p):\langle P\bmod p\rangle]
=\frac{\#E(\mathbf F_p)}{\operatorname{ord}(P\bmod p)}.
\]

This is **not frozen**. Noncyclicity of \(E(\mathbf F_p)\) may indicate either that the index is informative or that it is the wrong invariant.

Needed future questions: saturation, Kummer/division fields detecting divisibility, adelic image, and precise Chebotarev conditions.

## Level 2: abelian variety / semiabelian variety

Replace \((E,P)\) by \((A,P)\) and later a pointed semiabelian group. Determine the right finite-reduction quotient/index invariant before defining any zeta product.

## Level 3: 1-motive

Candidate organizing object:
\[
M_P=[\mathbf Z\to G],\qquad 1\mapsto P.
\]

Possible future goal: an intrinsic local statistic measuring the index or saturation of the lattice-generated reduction inside the finite group attached to the reduction of \(M_P\).

## Realization questions

- \(\ell\)-adic;

interpretations.

No Hodge or motivic significance is presumed. A negative answer is a valid outcome.
