# Prior-Art / Novelty Audit Plan

**Status: planned, not executed.**

The comprehensive audit begins only after a foundations pass.

## Central question

Do established generalized-Artin, residual-index, Chebotarev, or Frobenian-Euler-product frameworks already contain the proposed object essentially for free?

Exact notation is irrelevant. Mathematical equivalence counts as overlap.

## Search fronts

### Residual index / Artin

Search residual index modulo primes, subgroup index generated by a fixed integer, generalized Artin conjectures, near-primitive roots, Stephens-type constants, average multiplicative order, index distributions, primitive-root densities, and orders modulo primes.

### Chebotarev / Kummer

Search radical and Kummer extensions \(\mathbf Q(\zeta_n,a^{1/n})\), division fields, Lenstra/Hooley/Wagstaff/Moree-style primitive-root machinery, entanglement corrections, and splitting criteria for residual index.

### Weighted Euler products

Search Frobenian functions and Euler products, Chebotarev Euler products, partial/prime-set zeta functions, Mertens theorems for Chebotarev sets, and Euler products with fractional poles or density exponents.

### General functions of the index

Search prime averages of \(f(I_a(p))\), moments, generating functions, Mellin/Dirichlet transforms, and residual-index distributions.

### Algebraic-group analogues

Search elliptic Artin conjectures, primitive points, orders of reduced rational points, generated-subgroup index in \(E(\mathbf F_p)\), abelian varieties, cyclic reduction, Kummer representations, support problems, semiabelian groups, and adelic division fields.

### 1-motives / realizations

Search \([\mathbf Z\to G]\) 1-motives, Kummer 1-motives, \(\ell\)-adic realizations of rational points, Mumford–Tate groups of 1-motives, Hodge realizations, and motivic Galois groups of pointed varieties.

## A–G overlap matrix

Every candidate source must be tested against:

A. residual-index distribution;  
B. general averages of \(f(I_a(p))\);  
C. the family \(f_z(n)=n^{-z}\);  
D. the two-variable Euler product;  
E. a continuously varying singularity exponent / “spectrum”;  
F. Kummer-tower / L-function factorization;  
G. pointed algebraic-group / 1-motive extension.

## Required record per source

Record:

- full citation and version/date;
- exact theorem/proposition/section;
- exact mathematical object;
- hypotheses;
- which of A–G it implies;
- whether the proposed formulation is only notationally different;
- unresolved mismatch;
- links/identifiers sufficient to reproduce the check.

Do not rely on abstracts when theorem text is available.
