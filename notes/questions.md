# Open Questions

This is a live question bank, not a conjecture list.

## Foundational

- What is the exact elementary equivalence between \(n\mid I_a(p)\) and a power-residue condition?
- Is complete splitting in \(K_n(a)\) exactly equivalent after excluding the correct finite set of primes?
- Which hypotheses on \(a\) are essential versus merely convenient?
- How do perfect powers change degrees and indicator formulas?
- Where do entanglement corrections enter: at individual \(n\), in infinite inclusion–exclusion, or both?

## Mean / spectrum

- For which real or complex \(z\) does \(\Delta_a(z)\) exist?
- Does generalized Artin theory already give it for a broad class of \(f\)?
- What is the correct Möbius transform of \(n^{-z}\), and what convergence does the resulting degree series have?
- Is \(z=0\) continuous from the right in the theoretical expansion?
- Under what conditions may \(z\to\infty\) pass through the degree series or the prime average?

## Euler product

- Does the \(k=1\) prime sum alone control the singular exponent at \(s=1\)?
- Is the remainder analytic and nonzero near \(s=1\)?
- Is the local factor too crude? Would a logarithmic derivative be more natural?
- Can the product be decomposed using Artin or Dedekind L-functions, perhaps only after truncation/inclusion–exclusion?
- Does a generic nonintegral \(\Delta_a(z)\) really manifest as a branch-type singularity?

## Object selection

- Is \(I^{-z}\) canonical, or merely the first Mellin-like probe?
- Is a generating series in the discrete residual index more fundamental?
- Is the full distribution of \(I_a(p)\) the actual object, with \(\Delta_a(z)\) just one transform?

## Generalisation

- What is the correct analogue of “index generated by a point” when the reduced finite group is noncyclic?
- Should one use subgroup index, saturation index, cokernel structure, or a vector of elementary divisors?
- Is the 1-motive \([\mathbf Z\to G]\) the natural home for the data?
- Does any Hodge/motivic structure constrain the local order statistic in a way not already encoded by the adelic representation?

## Novelty

- Which of A–G are already theorems in different notation?
- Is the two-variable Euler product itself a useful layer, or only packaging?
- If the \(\mathbf G_m\) object collapses, does an elliptic/1-motivic analogue remain independently interesting?
