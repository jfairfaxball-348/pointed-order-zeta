# Open Questions

This is a live question bank, not a conjecture list.

## Foundational — resolved in Session 2

- For \(p\nmid a\),
  \[
  n\mid I_a(p)
  \iff
  n\mid p-1\text{ and }a\bmod p\in(\mathbf F_p^\times)^n.
  \]
- For fixed \(n\) and \(p\nmid an\),
  \[
  n\mid I_a(p)
  \iff
  p\text{ splits completely in }K_n(a)=\mathbf Q(\zeta_n,a^{1/n}).
  \]
- The non-perfect-power hypothesis is not needed for the individual splitting equivalence; it affects degree formulas and later constants.
- Negative bases require no correction in the individual criterion.
- Entanglement is not an exception to the fixed-\(n\) criterion; it enters degree factorisations, simultaneous conditions, and tower-level inclusion–exclusion.
- The naive shorthand “\(a\) is an \(n\)-th power mod \(p\)” without \(n\mid p-1\) is false as an equivalent formulation.

## Session 3 — prior art and Kummer degrees

- Which generalized-Artin, residual-index, and near-primitive-root theorems already state the fixed-\(n\) density or more general residual-index distributions?
- Has the average of a general arithmetic function \(f(I_a(p))\) already been treated via Möbius inversion and Kummer degrees?
- What exact formulas and uniform lower bounds are known for
  \[
  [\mathbf Q(\zeta_n,a^{1/n}):\mathbf Q]
  \]
  for non-perfect-power and perfect-power \(a\)?
- Exactly how are entanglement corrections formulated, and when do they obstruct naive Euler factorisation?
- Which results justify
  \[
  \operatorname{average}_p f(I_a(p))
  =
  \sum_{n\ge1}\frac{(\mu*f)(n)}{[K_n(a):\mathbf Q]}?
  \]
- Are these results unconditional, GRH-conditional, or dependent on the class of \(f\)?

## Mean / spectrum

- For which real or complex \(z\) does \(\Delta_a(z)\) exist?
- Does generalized Artin theory already give it for \(f_z(n)=n^{-z}\), or for a much broader class?
- Is the continuous \(z\)-parameter substantive or just a Mellin-like repackaging of a known residual-index distribution?
- Under what conditions may \(z\to\infty\) pass through the degree series or prime average?

## Euler product

- Does the \(k=1\) prime sum control the singular exponent at \(s=1\)?
- Is the remainder analytic and nonzero near \(s=1\)?
- Would a logarithmic derivative be more natural than the frozen local factor?
- Can the product be decomposed using Artin or Dedekind \(L\)-functions?
- Does a generic nonintegral \(\Delta_a(z)\) produce a branch-type singularity?

## Object selection

- Is \(I^{-z}\) canonical, or merely a first Mellin-like probe?
- Is a generating series in the discrete residual index more fundamental?
- Is the full distribution of \(I_a(p)\) the actual object, with \(\Delta_a(z)\) just one transform?

## Generalisation — deferred

- What is the right analogue of “index generated by a point” when the reduced finite group is noncyclic?
- Should one use subgroup index, saturation index, cokernel structure, or elementary divisors?
- Is the 1-motive \([\mathbf Z\to G]\) the natural home for the data?
- Does any Hodge/motivic structure add information not already encoded by the adelic representation?

## Novelty

- Which project layers A–G are already theorems in different notation?
- Is the two-variable Euler product a useful layer or only packaging?
- If the \(\mathbf G_m\) packaging is already implicit in generalized Artin theory, is any nontrivial structural layer left to develop?
